# Author Biographies

The Journal of Kinesiology and Wellness (JKW) requires an author biography for **every author** listed on a manuscript. Biographies must be provided before a submission can continue through processing, so preparing them in advance helps avoid delays.

## Formatting Options

Choose either of the two formats below. You may use whichever you prefer, but use the **same format consistently for all authors** on the manuscript.

### Format 1 — Concise

Name, credentials, and institution, followed by title.

> Steven Ross Murray, DA, MS, University of California, Berkeley
> Professor of Teaching and Director, Physical Education Program, Division of Biological Sciences

### Format 2 — Narrative

Name and institution, followed by a brief prose bio.

> Duane Knudson, Texas State University
>
> Duane V. Knudson, Ph.D., is a Regents' Professor and University Distinguished Professor in the Department of Health & Human Performance at Texas State University. His research addresses the biomechanics of sport and exercise, qualitative movement diagnosis, and research quality in kinesiology.

## Submitting Your Biographies

- Provide a biography for each author listed on the manuscript.
- Use a single, consistent format across all authors.
- Include biographies with your submission materials to keep your manuscript moving through processing.
